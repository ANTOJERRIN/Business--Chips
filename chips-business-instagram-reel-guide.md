# Instagram Reel Strategy Guide — Vegetable Chips Business

## 1. Reel Concept Ideas (Pick One to Start)
- **ASMR Crunch** — close-up of chips being poured, stacked, or bitten, crunch sound as the star
- **Process / Behind-the-Scenes** — slicing, frying, seasoning shown in quick cuts
- **Before/After Transformation** — raw vegetable → finished chip
- **Trending Audio Sync** — product visuals synced to a currently trending sound
- **POV/Relatable Hook** — e.g. "POV: you opened the wrong bag of chips and now you can't stop"

👉 Start with ASMR or Process style — easiest to shoot, performs well for food pages.

---

## 2. The First 1–2 Seconds (Hook)
- Close-up action shot (chip breaking, oil sizzling, seasoning sprinkle), OR
- Bold on-screen text hook (e.g. "5 vegetables you didn't know make amazing chips")

If it doesn't grab attention instantly, people scroll — and the algorithm stops pushing it.

---

## 3. Shooting Checklist
- Shoot **vertical (9:16)**
- Use natural light or a simple ring light
- Get 3–4 short clips:
  - Close-up texture shot
  - Hands doing an action (pouring, sprinkling, packing)
  - Wide shot of the setup
  - "Money shot" — final product, bag opened, or a bite
- Keep each clip 2–4 seconds in the final edit

### Natural "Eating Order" Shot Sequence (Recommended Structure)
1. **Bag Reveal** — unsealing/opening the pack
2. **Pour Shot** — chips falling into a bowl/plate
3. **Pick-Up Shot** — hand grabbing a chip
4. **Bite/Crunch Shot** — the money shot, close-up, good sound
5. **Reaction/Repeat** — going back for more, or bowl emptying fast

This mirrors real eating behavior, which makes it relatable and craving-inducing.

---

## 4. Using Trending Audio
- Instagram → tap **+ → Reel** → tap audio icon → browse **Reels > Trending Audio**
- Small upward arrow next to a sound = currently trending
- Match your best visual (chip snap, bag reveal) to the beat drop

---

## 5. Editing Tips
- Use Instagram's built-in editor or **CapCut** (free, popular for this style)
- Keep total length **7–15 seconds** — short reels loop and get rewatched
- Add on-screen captions/text (many watch on mute)
- End with a clear CTA: "DM to order," "Link in bio," or location tag

---

## 6. Before Posting
- Write a caption: hook line + product info + soft CTA
- Use **3–5 relevant hashtags** (not 30) — e.g. #veggiechips #healthysnacks #[yourcity]food
- Tag your **location** (important for local food business)
- Post when your audience is active (early evening usually works for snacks)

---

## 7. Track What Works (Simple, No Tools Needed)
After posting, check **View Insights** on each reel:
- **Saves** — did people bookmark it?
- **Shares** — did people send it to a friend?
- **Watch time/replays** — did people watch till the end or loop it?

Whichever reel gets the most saves/shares = repeat that style. That's the whole "tracking" process — no technical setup required.

---

## 8. Posting Consistency
- Post **3–5 reels a week** using similar formats
- Refine based on what performs best (saves/shares > likes)
- One reel rarely goes viral alone — consistency + refinement is what works

---

## 9. Instagram Automation (Optional — Not Required to Start)

### What automation tools do:
- Auto-post reels/photos on a schedule
- Auto-reply to DMs/comments (e.g., "price?" → auto price list)
- Analytics dashboard in one place

### When to consider it:
- Posting often enough that scheduling saves real time
- Getting repetitive DMs ("how much?", "do you deliver?")
- Want all metrics in one place

### When to hold off:
- Just starting out — content quality/hook matters more than automation right now
- You want a personal touch in DMs (builds trust for small food businesses)
